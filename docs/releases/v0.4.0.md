# solseer v0.4.0 — presence-aware joins and biome OCR

This release connects signal detection to the Roblox client more reliably, adds current-server awareness, and introduces local fullscreen OCR for biome detection and Play-screen automation.

## Current server and safer automatic joins

- Authenticated account presence identifies the exact Sol's RNG Job ID currently occupied by the configured account.
- A biome-colored current-server card shows the server, username, last detected biome, connection state, and join cooldown.
- Current servers are marked throughout the interface and excluded from manual rejoin and automatic server selection.
- Every join begins a 60-second Auto-join cooldown while Roblox loads; Auto-Start can end it early after Play and a later biome reading confirm startup.
- Join history can label attempts as **Rare biome**, **Not rare**, or **Unmarked**, retaining population evidence for future analysis.

## Fullscreen biome OCR and Auto-Start

- A bundled Windows helper continuously reads the biome region while Roblox is foreground and maximized, even when Auto-Start is off.
- Settings now includes calibrated **1920 × 1080** and **2560 × 1440** OCR profiles.
- The last successful biome remains visible through unclear or unavailable later reads.
- User-selected target biomes pause Auto-join until the account leaves that biome.
- Optional Auto-Start fuzzy-matches the Play button, stimulates Roblox's hover state with a short mouse sweep, and clicks through Windows `SendInput`.
- When enabled and biome text stays unclear, Auto-Start taps `O` to zoom out, with safeguards that suppress input on the Play screen and cap repeated presses.

## Faster, clearer signals

- Authenticated polling now refreshes the top server page every two seconds and removes servers from the UI after they become stale.
- Early and Rapid bursts share an exact 15.5-second window: Early requires +2 and Rapid requires +3 or more.
- Rapid bursts remain actionable at 20/20 for moving queues and display a Full indicator.
- Burst signals remain visible until at least half of the original population gain has left.
- Signal labels, supporting text, toolbar layout, and current-server styling now stay consistent across views.

## Updating

1. Stop the old app with **Ctrl+C**.
2. Download **solseer-v0.4.0-windows-x64.zip** and extract the entire ZIP into a new folder.
3. Open the `solseer` folder and double-click **Start solseer.cmd**.

The OCR helper is unsigned and Windows-only. It does not inject into Roblox, and mouse or keyboard input remains disabled unless Auto-Start is explicitly enabled. This release passes **171 automated tests**, the production build, native helper compilation, and Windows package verification.
